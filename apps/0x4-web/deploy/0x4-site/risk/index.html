Key points

01Before you begin

This disclosure describes the principal risks of holding, transferring and trading digital assets through 0x4. It forms part of our Terms of Service. It cannot list every risk, and new risks appear as technology and regulation change. Read it carefully, and consider whether these activities suit your knowledge, experience and financial position.

02Market risk

Digital asset prices are highly volatile and can fall sharply or to zero, at any hour and without warning. Many tokens, especially newly launched ones, have thin liquidity: a single large order can move the price, you may be unable to sell at the price shown, and slippage can be significant. Charts, rankings and historical returns describe the past and do not predict the future.

03Leverage and liquidation

Perpetual contracts use leverage, which magnifies both gains and losses. At high leverage, a price move of a few percent against you can trigger liquidation, closing your position and taking the margin behind it. Positions held on cross margin share one balance, so a loss on one position can liquidate the others. Liquidation is executed automatically by the protocol and does not wait for you. Funding payments are exchanged between long and short positions while you hold them and can erode a position over time. Fast markets, delayed prices or network congestion can cause liquidation at worse prices than expected.

04Keys and custody

A self-custodial wallet gives you full control and full responsibility. If you lose your recovery phrase and your device, your assets are lost permanently. If someone else obtains your recovery phrase or private keys, or gains control of your unlocked device, they can move your assets instantly. Malware, compromised devices, cloud backups of screenshots and careless storage are common causes of loss.

05Scams and phishing

Fraudsters impersonate support staff, well-known people and projects, including 0x4. They build fake websites and apps, and they request signatures that hand over control of your tokens. 0x4 will never ask for your recovery phrase or private keys, and will never ask you to send funds to verify your account. Before you sign, check the website, the contract and what the signature allows. Be wary of unsolicited messages, giveaways and offers that promise guaranteed returns.

06Tokens you may encounter

Anyone can create a token. Tokens shown in 0x4, including trending tokens and tokens mentioned by other users, have not been reviewed by us. Some are designed to deceive: they copy the name of a real project, restrict selling, hide fees or allow their creators to mint or withdraw liquidity. Security labels and warnings in the Services are imperfect and may miss problems or flag tokens incorrectly.

07Smart contracts and protocols

Swaps, perpetuals and gifts rely on smart contracts and decentralized protocols. Code can contain undiscovered vulnerabilities, upgrades and governance decisions can change how a protocol behaves, and attacks can drain funds. Price oracles can report stale or manipulated values, which may cause unexpected execution or liquidation. Neither 0x4 nor the protocols guarantee that funds lost in such events will be recovered.

08Bridges and cross-chain transfers

Moving assets between blockchains relies on bridges and intermediaries, which add their own technical and counterparty risk and have historically been frequent targets of attack. Cross-chain transfers can be delayed, can arrive as a different token than expected, or can fail and require manual recovery.

09Stablecoins

Stablecoins aim to hold a fixed value but can lose their peg, sometimes abruptly. Their issuers may freeze or block addresses, and their reserves may be insufficient or unavailable. Energy is purchased with USDT, so the risks of that stablecoin apply to the funds you deposit.

10Networks and fees

Blockchain networks can congest, halt, fork or reorganize. Network fees can rise sharply, and a transaction can fail while still consuming fees. Transactions waiting to be confirmed may be front-run or executed at a different price. Confirmed transactions cannot be reversed. Over the longer term, advances in computing, including quantum computing, may weaken the cryptography that blockchains rely on.

11Sprites and automation

Sprites make decisions through an experimental simulation of a biological brain, and their conversation is generated by AI models. Neither has been proven to trade profitably. A sprite can misread the market, trade repeatedly into losses or behave in ways its owner did not anticipate. With automatic trading enabled, trades are executed while you may not be watching. Keep limits small, review activity often, and revoke the permission whenever you are unsure. Rankings of sprites reflect past activity only.

12Energy and gifts

USDT deposited for energy cannot be withdrawn or refunded, and energy has no value outside gifting in the Services. If the Services become unavailable, unused energy may become unusable. Gifts depend on a smart contract and on scheduled settlement, which may be delayed by network conditions or contract issues. Only acquire energy you intend to spend.

13Social content

Posts, chats, streams and rankings are created by users who may be wrong, biased or paid to promote an asset, and they may hold the assets they discuss. Following others’ trades or calls exposes you to their mistakes and their motives. Popularity is not evidence of quality.

14Legal, regulatory and tax

Laws governing digital assets differ between countries and change often. A new law, enforcement action or sanction can restrict or end access to a feature, a token or the Services in your location. Some activities may be unlawful where you are, and it is your responsibility to check. Transactions may create tax obligations, and records on public blockchains are permanent.

15No protection scheme

Digital assets held in your wallet are not bank deposits. They are not covered by deposit insurance, investor compensation schemes or similar public protections. If assets are lost, stolen or devalued, there is generally no fund or authority that will make you whole.

01开始之前

02市场风险

数字资产价格波动剧烈，可能在任何时刻毫无预兆地大幅下跌，甚至归零。许多代币，尤其是新发行的代币，流动性很浅：一笔大单就能推动价格，您可能无法按显示的价格卖出，滑点也可能很大。图表、排名与历史收益描述的是过去，不能预示未来。

03杠杆与强制平仓

永续合约使用杠杆，收益与亏损会被同等放大。在高杠杆下，价格反向波动几个百分点就可能触发强制平仓，您的仓位会被关闭，背后的保证金随之亏损。全仓模式下各仓位共用同一份余额，一个仓位的亏损可能连带其他仓位被强平。强平由协议自动执行，不会等待您操作。持仓期间多空双方之间会交换资金费，长期持有可能持续侵蚀仓位。行情剧烈、价格延迟或网络拥堵时，强平价格可能比预期更差。

04密钥与自托管

自托管钱包让您拥有完全的控制权，也承担完全的责任。助记词和设备同时丢失，资产将永久无法找回。他人一旦取得您的助记词、私钥，或控制了您已解锁的设备，即可立即转走您的资产。恶意软件、被入侵的设备、截图被同步到云端以及保管不慎，都是常见的损失原因。

05诈骗与钓鱼

诈骗者会冒充客服、知名人士和项目方，包括冒充 0x4。他们会搭建虚假的网站与应用，并诱导您签署交出代币控制权的签名。0x4 永远不会向您索要助记词或私钥，也永远不会要求您转账来验证账户。签名前，请核对网站、合约以及这次签名允许做什么。对不请自来的消息、空投赠送以及承诺稳赚的机会，请保持警惕。

06您可能遇到的代币

任何人都可以发行代币。0x4 中展示的代币，包括热门代币和其他用户提到的代币，均未经我们审核。其中一些专为欺骗而设计：仿冒真实项目的名称、限制卖出、隐藏费用，或允许发行者增发、撤走流动性。本服务中的安全标签与提示并不完善，可能遗漏问题，也可能误判。

07智能合约与协议

兑换、永续合约与礼物都依赖智能合约和去中心化协议。代码可能存在尚未发现的漏洞，升级与治理决定可能改变协议的运作方式，攻击可能导致资金被盗。价格预言机可能报出过时或被操纵的数值，从而造成意外的成交或强平。0x4 与各协议均不保证在此类事件中损失的资金能够追回。

08跨链桥与跨链转账

在区块链之间转移资产依赖跨链桥与中间方，它们各自带有技术风险与对手方风险，历来是攻击的高发目标。跨链转账可能延迟到账，可能以与预期不同的代币到账，也可能失败而需要人工处理。

09稳定币

稳定币以保持固定价值为目标，但可能突然脱锚。发行方可以冻结或封禁地址，其储备也可能不足或无法动用。能量以 USDT 购买，因此该稳定币的风险同样适用于您充入的资金。

10网络与费用

区块链网络可能拥堵、停机、分叉或重组。网络费可能急剧上涨，交易失败时也照样消耗费用。等待确认的交易可能被抢跑，或以不同的价格成交。已确认的交易无法撤回。从更长远看，包括量子计算在内的算力进步，可能削弱区块链所依赖的密码学。

11小精灵与自动交易

小精灵通过对生物大脑的实验性模拟作出决策，它的对话由 AI 模型生成，两者都没有被证明能够稳定盈利。小精灵可能误判行情、反复亏损，或做出主人没有预料到的行为。开启自动交易后，交易会在您可能没有关注的时候执行。请把额度设小，经常查看记录，有任何疑虑就撤销授权。小精灵的排名只反映过去的表现。

为获取能量而充入的 USDT 不可提现、不可退还，能量在本服务的送礼功能之外没有价值。如本服务无法继续提供，未使用的能量可能无法再用。礼物依赖智能合约与定期结算，可能因网络状况或合约问题延迟。请只获取您打算用掉的能量。

动态、聊天、直播与排名由用户产生，他们可能判断错误、立场有偏，或收了钱推广某项资产，也可能本人就持有所谈论的资产。跟随他人的交易或喊单，意味着同时承担他们的失误与动机。热度不能证明质量。

14法律、监管与税务

您钱包中的数字资产不是银行存款，不受存款保险、投资者赔偿计划或类似公共保障的保护。资产一旦丢失、被盗或贬值，通常没有任何基金或机构会为您弥补损失。
