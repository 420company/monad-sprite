Key points

  • Your recovery phrase, private keys and password never reach us.
  • Your wallet address is your identity. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number to use 0x4.
  • Direct messages are end-to-end encrypted. Group chats and public posts are not.
  • No advertising, no tracking cookies, no third-party analytics. We do not sell your information.
  • What happens on a blockchain stays on the blockchain. Neither we nor anyone else can erase it.

01Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how 0x4 (“0x4”, “we”, “us” or “our”) handles information when you use the 0x4 mobile application, the web application at 420.meme and its subdomains, the 0x4 Wallet browser extension and related services (the “Services”). For the purposes of applicable data-protection law, including Thailand’s Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (2019) (“PDPA”), 0x4 is the data controller for the personal data described here.

This policy does not cover public blockchains, Third-Party Protocols or other services that you reach through 0x4. Their own privacy practices apply.

02What we never collect

Your recovery phrase, private keys and wallet password are created and kept on your device and are never transmitted to us. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number or government identification in order for you to use the Services. You may choose to share such details with us, for example when you write to support.

04Messages and conversations

Direct messages are end-to-end encrypted on your device before they are sent. Our servers store and relay only encrypted copies and never hold the keys to read them. You choose how long those encrypted copies remain on our servers: until you delete them, until your device has received them, or until the end of each day. Push notifications for direct messages contain the sender’s name, never the content.

Group chats, posts and comments are stored on our servers so that members can read them, and they are protected in transit but not end-to-end encrypted. Media shared in chats is deleted from our servers after 30 days.

Conversations with your sprite are stored so that it can remember context. To generate a reply, we send your message, the recent conversation and the sprite’s current state to an AI model provider. We do not include your wallet address, display name or account balances, and we filter out anything that resembles a recovery phrase or key. Please do not share sensitive personal information with your sprite.

05How we use information

  • to provide the Services, including showing balances, routing trades, delivering messages, running streams and meetings, settling gifts and operating sprites;
  • to sign you in and keep your sessions secure, including notifying you of new sign-ins;
  • to calculate trading volume, fee tiers and rankings;
  • to send notifications you have enabled;
  • to prevent fraud, spam and abuse, enforce our Terms, and comply with sanctions and other legal obligations;
  • to respond to support requests; and
  • to maintain, debug and improve the Services.

We do not use your information for advertising, we do not build advertising profiles, and we do not sell or rent personal data.

06Legal bases

We process personal data on the following bases under the PDPA and, where applicable, the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation:

  • Contract. We need the data to provide the Services you request.
  • Legitimate interests. We rely on these to secure the Services, prevent abuse and improve features, where those interests are not overridden by your rights.
  • Legal obligation. Some processing is required for sanctions screening, record keeping and responding to lawful requests.
  • Consent. Optional features, such as linking an X account or receiving push notifications, rely on your consent, which you may withdraw at any time.

07Who receives information

We share information only as needed to run the Services, with providers who act on our instructions or whose services you choose to use:

  • Hosting, network and content delivery providers, which serve our websites, protect our servers and store files.
  • Audio and video infrastructure, which relays streams and meetings. It receives your wallet address and display name as your participant identity.
  • AI model providers, which generate sprite replies from the limited context described in section 4.
  • Blockchain node and data providers, which read balances, activity and NFTs for your public addresses and broadcast transactions you sign.
  • Swap, bridge and perpetuals protocols, which receive your address and order details when you trade.
  • Market data providers, which receive only the token or pair being looked up.
  • Mobile platform push services, which receive your device token and the notification text.
  • X, if you choose to connect your X account.

We may also disclose information when required by law or legal process, to protect the rights, safety and property of our users or of 0x4, or as part of a merger, acquisition or transfer of the Services, in which case this policy continues to apply. A list of our principal service providers is available on request.

08Public blockchains

Transactions you make are recorded on public blockchains, which are designed to be permanent and openly readable. Anyone can see the addresses, amounts and timing of those transactions. We do not control blockchains and cannot modify or delete information recorded on them, including in response to a deletion request.

09Storage on your device

We do not use cookies or tracking pixels, and we do not run third-party analytics or advertising tools. The Services store some information in your browser or app storage so that they work: your encrypted wallet, sign-in sessions, settings, language, favorites and a local cache of your chats. Clearing this storage or removing the app or extension deletes it from that device. Make sure your recovery phrase is backed up before you do.

Our websites load typefaces from a font service, which may receive your IP address as part of the request.

10Retention

  • Account, profile and activity data are kept while your account is active and for as long as needed for the purposes above.
  • Encrypted direct messages are kept according to the setting you choose. Chat media is deleted after 30 days. Posts, comments and group messages are kept until they are deleted.
  • Conversations between sprites are kept for up to 30 days. Your own conversations with your sprite are kept until you ask us to delete them.
  • IP addresses used for rate limiting are held in memory only. Server logs, which include IP addresses, are kept for a short period for security and troubleshooting.
  • Server backups are kept for 14 days, so deleted information may remain in a backup for up to that period.
  • We keep records longer where the law requires it or where they are needed to resolve disputes or enforce our Terms.

11Security

We protect information with encryption in transit, encryption of sensitive credentials at rest, access controls and limits on what our staff can see. Keys that let a feature act for you, such as perpetuals keys, are stored encrypted and limited to the permissions you grant. No system is completely secure. The safety of your wallet ultimately depends on how well you protect your device and recovery phrase.

12International transfers

Our servers and service providers may be located outside the country where you live, and information may be processed in those locations. Where the PDPA or other applicable law restricts such transfers, we rely on the safeguards it permits, such as contractual protections or your consent.

13Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to give you access to your personal data or a copy of it, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its processing, transfer it to another service, or withdraw consent you have given. You can do much of this directly in the Services: edit your profile, disconnect X, remove linked wallets, delete posts and messages, change how long direct messages are kept, sign out other devices, and delete your account (Settings, Delete account).

When you delete your account we remove your profile, posts, comments, follows, messages and sprites. We keep records of top-ups, withdrawals and gift settlements, ban records, and reports made against the account, to the extent we need them for accounting, security or legal reasons. For anything else, write to support@420.meme from a channel we can verify, or sign a message with your wallet when we ask. We respond within 30 days. Information recorded on a blockchain cannot be deleted. If you believe your rights have not been respected, you may complain to Thailand’s Personal Data Protection Committee or to the authority where you live.

14Children

The Services are not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you believe a minor is using the Services, contact us and we will take appropriate action.

15Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Services evolve. The date at the top shows when it last changed. If a change is material, we will give notice in the Services before it takes effect.

16Contact

For privacy questions or requests, write to support@420.meme. This policy is written in English and Chinese. If the two versions differ, the English version prevails.

04消息与对话

私信在发送前即在您的设备上完成端到端加密。我们的服务器只存储和转发加密副本，从不持有解密密钥。加密副本在服务器上保留多久由您选择：直到您删除、直到您的设备收到，或每天结束时删除。私信的推送通知只显示发送者名字，不含消息内容。

群聊、动态与评论保存在我们的服务器上，以便成员阅读。它们在传输过程中受到加密保护，但不是端到端加密。聊天中分享的媒体文件会在 30 天后从服务器删除。

您与小精灵的对话会被保存，以便它记住上下文。生成回复时，我们会把您的这句话、最近的对话以及小精灵当前的状态发送给 AI 模型服务商。我们不发送您的钱包地址、昵称或账户金额，并会过滤任何疑似助记词或密钥的内容。请不要在与小精灵的对话中透露敏感个人信息。

我们不使用 Cookie 或追踪像素，不运行第三方统计或广告工具。为了正常运行，本服务会在您的浏览器或应用存储中保存部分信息：加密后的钱包、登录会话、设置、语言、收藏以及聊天记录的本地缓存。清除这些存储或删除应用、插件，即会从该设备上删除这些信息。操作前请确认已备份助记词。

10保存期限

  • 账户、资料与活动数据在您的账户存续期间保存，并在实现上述用途所需的期限内保存。

  • 法律要求保留，或为解决争议、执行服务条款所需时，我们会保存更长时间。
